Hemoglobin (Hb) is the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen.

Reference range (adult, non‑pregnant, male): 13.5 – 17.5 g/dL
Reference range (adult, female): 12.0 – 15.5 g/dL
Low Hb (anemia): May indicate iron deficiency, chronic disease, or blood loss. Symptoms can include fatigue, pallor, and shortness of breath.
High Hb: Could be due to dehydration, high altitude, smoking, or polycythemia.
Interpretation: Values outside the range suggest a need for further clinical evaluation, but are not diagnostic on their own.
