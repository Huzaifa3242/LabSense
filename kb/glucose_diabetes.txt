Fasting plasma glucose measures blood sugar after an overnight fast.

Reference range (adult, fasting): 70 – 99 mg/dL
Impaired fasting glucose (100‑125 mg/dL): May indicate pre‑diabetes.
Diabetic range (≥126 mg/dL): Suggestive of diabetes mellitus; confirm with repeat testing.
Low glucose (<70 mg/dL): Can cause hypoglycemia symptoms such as shakiness, sweating, and confusion; may be due to medication, fasting, or endocrine disorders.
Interpretation: Values should be considered with patient symptoms and risk factors.
