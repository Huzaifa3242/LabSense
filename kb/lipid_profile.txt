Lipid profile assessment includes total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, and triglycerides.

- **Reference ranges (adult, fasting):**
  - Total cholesterol: <200 mg/dL (desirable)
  - LDL‑C: <100 mg/dL (optimal), 100‑129 mg/dL (near optimal), 130‑159 mg/dL (borderline high), 160‑189 mg/dL (high), ≥190 mg/dL (very high)
  - HDL‑C: ≥60 mg/dL (protective), 40‑59 mg/dL (acceptable), <40 mg/dL (increased risk)
  - Triglycerides: <150 mg/dL (normal), 150‑199 mg/dL (borderline high), 200‑499 mg/dL (high), ≥500 mg/dL (very high)

- **Interpretation:**
  - High LDL or low HDL suggests increased cardiovascular risk.
  - Elevated triglycerides can be associated with metabolic syndrome, diabetes, or excessive alcohol intake.
  - Values should be interpreted with patient risk factors (age, sex, smoking, blood pressure, etc.).

- **Disclaimer:** Educational summary only; not a diagnostic conclusion.
